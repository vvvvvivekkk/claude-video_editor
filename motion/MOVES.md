# The 10 moves

Reference for the named motion moves Claude knows. When you ask for one by
name ("add a count-up at 12s", "use the reframe move here"), HyperFrames
knows what to do.

Taken from Damiano Caudullo's "Motion Graphics with Claude Code" (page 7),
distilled for use in this repo's overlay stage.

| Move           | What it does                                                     | When to use                                                   |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| rise           | Fades in while moving up a little.                                | Default card entrance. Calm, non-distracting.                 |
| pop            | Scales up from small with a small overshoot.                      | A single word/stat landing on the beat.                       |
| count-up       | A number rolls to its value.                                      | Any stat you want the viewer to feel.                         |
| checklist tick | Lines get a green tick one by one.                                | "Three things that doubled my sales" — the list payoff.       |
| typewriter     | Text types itself.                                                | A quote, a tagline, a code-like reveal.                       |
| slide-in       | Comes in from the side.                                           | Chaining several cards without them stacking visually.        |
| blur-in        | Blurry to sharp.                                                  | Soft reveal when you don't want a hard punch.                 |
| punch-in       | The whole frame zooms in fast.                                    | On the line people should remember. Pair with the hook.       |
| reframe        | Your video shrinks into a card and something new appears.         | Transitioning from face-to-camera to a stat or screenshot.    |
| bar fill       | A progress bar fills up.                                          | "Uploading… 94%" moments, before/after, ratios.               |

## Composition rules

- One style across the whole video — mix the moves, don't mix the styles.
- Each move starts on the exact word, not on the second. Say the moment in
  words ("when I say *forty a week*"), not "at 16.8s".
- Keep moves out of the bottom third (that's where captions burn in) and
  off the speaker's face.
