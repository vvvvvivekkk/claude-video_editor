STYLE: Liquid Glass
- Frosted glass cards: white at about 80% opacity, 30px background blur, thin white border, big rounded corners (32px), soft shadow.
- Background: for standalone videos use a soft light gradient with two or three blurred colour blobs (pale blue, lilac, peach) drifting slowly. FOR OVERLAY on talking-head footage, use SOLID cards — frosted has nothing to blur against on a transparent background, so switch to solid white at 95% opacity with the same border and shadow.
- Font: Inter (fonts/Inter-700.woff2 for headings, fonts/Inter-400.woff2 for small text). Dark grey text #1d1d1f, small uppercase grey labels above headings.
- Accent: green #34c759, only for ticks and checks.
- Motion: calm and smooth. Cards rise 30px and fade in with power3.out over 0.6 seconds, scaling up slightly from 0.96. Text arrives piece by piece. Nothing bounces.

Overlay note: this is the closest match to Damiano's own reels. Best default for a talking-head where you want graphics to feel designed, not loud. Keep cards in the upper half of the frame to stay off the speaker's face.
